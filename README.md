## 📝 1. Prompt Used
The following structured prompt was utilized to guide the AI model:

Role: You are an expert AI Performance marketer and UGC Ad Scriptwriter speciality in creating short film ads for Instagram reels, Facebook and YouTube shorts.

Context: Create a complete UGC ad for the product "Electric Trimmer" targeting only male audiences aged 15 to 40.

Task:
1) 3 Hooks: Generate 3 different scroll stopping hooks for the first 3 seconds addressing common problems (example - Bad shaving, skin cutting damage from blade trimmer).

2) 3 UGC video Ad scripts (30-45 seconds each):  • Two column layout: [Camera shot] and [Voiceover/ On screen text].
  • Follow the structure: Hook→Problem→Solution→Result→Call to Action(CTA).
  • Keep the language natural, authentic and conversation (not salesy and corporate)

3) Platform Caption and CTA: Write 3 captions for instagram reels and YouTube shorts with hashtags and call to action (CTA) link placeholder for direct order/booking.

Formatting & Tone:
• Tone should be related, causal and friendly.
• Including visual cues (Example: Direct speaking to front camera, Pointing to face, Showing Beard Before and After result

4. 🎨 Creative Direction & Production Guidelines

To make the advertisements look authentic and engaging, follow these guidelines:

- Camera Style: Use a smartphone front camera for direct-to-camera shots.
- Lighting: Use natural lighting for clear product demonstrations.
- Editing: Add quick cuts, smooth transitions, and subtitles.
- Visual Demonstration: Show the product in action and include genuine before-and-after footage.
- Audio: Use natural conversational voiceovers.
- Duration: Keep each advertisement between 30–45 seconds.
- CTA Placement: Display the product link or ordering instruction at the end.
